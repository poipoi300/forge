I want a "what can I cook tonight" feature. The idea is I keep a rough list of what's in my pantry right now, just a simple list I update whenever I feel like it, and then the app tells me which recipes I can actually make with what I have.

Even better if it can also show me recipes I'm close to being able to make, like if I'm only missing one or two ingredients, so I know what to grab next time I'm at the store. It should be smart about matching too. If I have "flour" in my pantry it should recognize that against a recipe that calls for "all-purpose flour", not treat them as totally different things. Same idea for stuff like "tomatoes" matching "roma tomatoes" or whatever, use your judgment on how far to take that.

Doesn't need any barcode scanning or grocery store integration, just a plain list I can edit by hand, and a page or section that shows me what's cookable or almost cookable right now.

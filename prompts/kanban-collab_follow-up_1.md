# Card ordering gets weird with simultaneous drags

We've been using the board for a couple weeks now and there's an annoying bug. When two of us drag cards in the same column around the same time, the ordering gets messed up — cards will clobber each other's position, and sometimes a card visibly jumps to a different spot right after you drop it, like someone else's drag overwrote yours.

It happens most in our "In Progress" column since that's where most of the team is working at once. Nobody's losing cards, it's just the order within the column that gets scrambled or reverts unexpectedly.

Can you make ordering stay stable and predictable even when multiple people are dragging cards in the same column at the same time? I don't want the column to lock up or block anyone while someone else is mid-drag, that would defeat the purpose. Just want drops to consistently land where they're supposed to and stay there.

# Follow-up: travel and streaks

I travel a decent amount for work and the streak counting is getting confused. A couple times I've checked off a habit late at night after midnight, meaning to log it for "yesterday", and it either didn't count for the right day or messed up my streak. I think part of the issue is timezones too — I'm not always in the same zone I was in when I set things up.

Can we make this behave sanely regardless of what timezone I happen to be in at the moment, and also let me explicitly check off a habit for a specific day instead of always assuming "right now"? Like if I forgot to log Tuesday's run and it's now Thursday, I want to be able to go back and mark Tuesday done without it acting weird.

Streaks should just reflect reality based on the days I actually did the thing, no matter when or where I happened to type the command.

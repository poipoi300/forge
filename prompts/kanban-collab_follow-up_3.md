# Client access with limited permissions

We're bringing a client onto one of our boards so they can follow along with progress, but I don't want them to have full run of it. Can we add roles per board — something like a viewer role that can leave comments on cards but can't move them or edit anything, versus our regular members who can do everything like normal.

Also while you're in there, it'd be great to have an activity feed on each card showing what's happened to it — who moved it, who changed the assignee, who commented, and when. I know we don't have that history for stuff that already happened, but going forward I'd like it to at least start building up from whatever point it ships, even if it can only partially piece together the older stuff.

We'll probably invite more outside people onto other boards later so figure the role thing should be board-specific rather than a single global setting for someone's account.
